# Project Brief — Him Over There

## One-line pitch

A mature, stylized 3D photography career and life simulator where you play as Him Over There, build a creative career from rejection and survival into business, art, fatherhood, education and legacy, while actually learning how photography works.

## Core fantasy

The player should feel like they are living the life of a working photographer—not merely selecting photography-themed menu options.

They should:
- Walk and drive through a Chicago-inspired city
- Meet people organically
- Receive client inquiries
- Negotiate prices and contracts
- Scout locations
- Pack gear
- Position lights
- Direct real-feeling subjects
- Make camera-setting decisions
- Take photos that reflect those decisions
- Cull and retouch
- Deliver work
- Handle money, bills, relationships and reputation
- Build a studio and creative identity

## Autobiographical spine

The canonical protagonist is Him Over There.

Story progression:

1. **No One Will Hire Me** — felony history and repeated employment rejection; photography becomes an available path.
2. **Him Over There** — early network: friends, models, exotic dancers, adult performers, musicians, nightlife and local creatives.
3. **Girl Dad** — fatherhood changes what money, time and ambition mean.
4. **The Photographer** — stronger portrait, beauty and boudoir work; people begin seeking him specifically.
5. **The Business** — studio, taxes, assistants, bigger clients, licensing, burnout and business systems.
6. **The Artist** — personal projects, fine art, exhibitions and voice.
7. **Legacy** — education, mentoring, major work and helping other creatives.

## Tone

- Mature
- Funny
- Chicago
- Stylish
- Grounded
- Culturally specific
- Occasionally ridiculous
- Never corporate or sterile
- Struggle without victimhood

## Photography education promise

A player who knows nothing about photography should become meaningfully better at:
- Exposure
- Focal length and perspective
- Focus
- Camera shake and motion blur
- ISO/noise tradeoffs
- Composition
- Lighting distance/softness/direction
- Posing and subject confidence
- Culling
- Retouching judgment
- Pricing/contracts/licensing
- Client communication

The game should teach primarily through consequences, not lectures.

## MVP success condition

The vertical slice is successful when a player can:

1. Start in the apartment.
2. Receive the first “How much you charge?” inquiry.
3. Negotiate/book the client.
4. Collect a deposit.
5. Plan/pack/travel.
6. Conduct a real interactive portrait/beauty/boudoir shoot.
7. Use exposure, focus, focal length and one-light controls.
8. Direct the subject.
9. Solve a mishap.
10. Capture photos with visibly different outcomes.
11. Cull and retouch.
12. Deliver.
13. Get paid/reviewed.
14. Progress toward the first studio.

## Long-term world direction

Do not build a giant city now. Grow from districts.

Future systems may include:
- More Chicago-inspired neighborhoods
- Family/home life
- Dating/relationships
- More photography genres
- Nightlife
- Agency/commercial work
- Gallery/fine art
- Teaching/workshops
- More vehicles
- More gear
- Dynamic weather/seasons
- Rival/collaborator photographers
- Expanded NPC memory and relationship arcs
